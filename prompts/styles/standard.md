# Standard Cover Letter Style

Use the classic professional cover letter structure:

## Structure (in order)

1. **Opening paragraph — Role Fit**
   Lead with the strongest factual match from `opening_hook`. State the role
   and company clearly. Convey genuine interest without superlatives.

2. **Technical / Experience paragraph**
   Cover the core technical requirements addressed in the content plan.
   Cite specific projects or roles from the evidence; use concrete nouns
   (tools, outcomes, contexts) rather than adjectives.

3. **Working Style / Collaboration paragraph** (if collaboration evidence exists)
   Address team-working, communication, or domain knowledge points from the
   plan. Keep it brief (2–3 sentences).

4. **Motivation / Closing paragraph**
   Use `closing_note` from the content plan to connect the applicant's
   interests to the specific company or role. One to two sentences.
   End with a forward-looking sentence (e.g. availability for interview).

## Style Notes

- Write in third-person-free, first-person voice (I, not "the applicant").
- Avoid empty qualifiers: "highly motivated", "passionate about", "strong
  experience in" — replace with concrete evidence-backed statements.
- Sentence length: vary between short (8–12 words) and medium (15–22 words).
  Avoid sentences over 30 words.
- Tone: professional and direct. Confident without arrogance.
- Do not use bullet points or headers in the letter body.
