# Senior Software Engineer — Acme Data GmbH

## About the role

We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our data infrastructure team
in Berlin. You will design and operate high-throughput data pipelines, collaborate
with data scientists, and help shape our engineering culture.

## What you'll do

- Design and maintain Python-based data pipelines processing large volumes of data
- Collaborate with data science and product teams to deliver reliable data products
- Mentor junior engineers and contribute to code review culture
- Work with cloud infrastructure (GCP or AWS)

## What we're looking for

- 5+ years of experience with Python in a production environment
- Experience with distributed data processing (Spark, Beam, or similar)
- Strong understanding of software engineering best practices (testing, CI/CD)
- Good communication skills and experience in cross-functional teams
- Experience with workflow orchestration (Airflow, Prefect, or similar)

## Nice to have

- Experience with dbt or similar transformation tools
- Familiarity with Kubernetes

## What we offer

- Competitive salary, 30 days holiday
- Remote-friendly, Berlin office available
- Strong engineering culture with regular tech talks
